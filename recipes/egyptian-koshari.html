Egyptian Koshari

Description

Egyptian Koshari is the ultimate comfort food and a beloved national dish. It is a hearty, flavorful combination of rice, macaroni, lentils, chickpeas, topped with crispy fried onions and a tangy, spicy tomato vinegar sauce (Daqqa).

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown lentils
  • 1 cup macaroni pasta
  • 1 cup small pasta (spaghetti broken into pieces)
  • 1 cup white rice
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced and fried until crispy
  • Tomato sauce and garlic vinegar (Daqqa)

Steps

  1. Cook the brown lentils in boiling water until tender, then set aside.
  2. Cook the rice and vermicelli together in a pot with oil and water.
  3. Boil the macaroni and spaghetti until al dente, then drain.
  4. Prepare the spiced tomato sauce and the garlic vinegar (Daqqa).
  5. Layer the dishes: start with a base of rice and pasta, add lentils and chickpeas, and top generously with crispy fried onions and tomato sauce.
